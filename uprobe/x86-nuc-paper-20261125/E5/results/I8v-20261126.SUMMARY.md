## Fork hang in W7t (pre-existing in Tern, not caused by careful mode or the view; recorded, not fixed)

W7t forks from the 3-thread target (main + the agent's IPC and detach threads) and creates a thread in the child. In
about half the attempts the child hangs at its first uprobe: the new thread enters Frida's interceptor
(`get_interceptor_thread_context` → `g_slice_alloc` → Gum's dlmalloc `mspace_malloc`) and spins forever in
`spin_acquire_lock` (gdb stacks: results/w7t-fork-hang-stacks.txt). The allocator's lock was held by another agent thread at fork
time and is inherited locked. Reproduced on plain Dn (c71a9987): 2 of 3 attempts hung; Dnextpc+CAREFUL 2/3; Dviewpc+CAREFUL
2/3 then 1/3; upstream Ol 0/3 (small sample). It affects any multithreaded target that forks and then starts a thread
that hits a probe before exec. Fix direction (not done here): a fork handler that takes Gum's allocator lock in prepare
and releases it in parent/child, or quiescing the agent's threads around fork.
