# Jetson E15 and E16, 2026-11-24

Both sessions: 16 B, nginx + wrk 100 conns, 10 s per leg, policy M, 2 rounds x 10 reps.
E15 180/180 rows valid (12:58-13:43 JST), E16 220/220 valid (13:44-14:36 JST).
K_wb32 / K_wb256 = kernel sslsniff-s4 with `SSLSNIFF_PB_WAKEUP=32` / `256`
(perf buffer sample_period = wakeup_events = N). Throughput of bpftime arms comes from the `-stock`
legs where they exist (gate (a) fallback). L3port has counting legs only.

- Exactness cost (paired): Dx-stock+EXACT - Dx-stock = 12.55 us/request [11.42, 13.68] = 6.27 us/event
  (counting legs: 6.10 us/event). x86: 1.507 us/event.
- Dx-stock+EXACT / K = 0.9016, / K_wb32 = 0.8088 (x86: 0.9909 vs K_wb32). Dx+EXACT is 16.2 us/request
  slower than K_wb32 [15.33, 17.03] and 8.3 us/request slower than stock K [7.48, 9.19].
- Equivalence Dx vs Dpc (switch off) at +-2 %: NOT shown. Dx/Dpc = 0.9783, 90 % CI [0.9668, 0.9900]
  (stock legs 0.9775 [0.9644, 0.9907]). With the switch off Dx is about 2.2 % slower than Dpc, so the
  exactness cost above is measured against Dx itself, not Dpc. Cause not investigated (the two patches
  add a check per identity use; code layout is the other candidate).
- Smoke (12:54): EXACT adds about 30 syscalls per request in the nginx worker (getpid +8, gettid +8,
  getuid +6, getgid +6, about +2 prctl), i.e. about 15 per event; 6.27 us / 15 = about 0.42 us per syscall.
- D-stock and K repeat E15 within 0.4 % (D 1.2010 vs 1.1999 GMR, K 13,133 vs 13,049 rps).
