## bpftime rejects injected PTX on `sm_103` (GB300) and does not build on CUDA 13

On an NVIDIA **GB300** (Blackwell Ultra, compute capability 10.3 / `sm_103`), Grace/aarch64, CUDA 13.2, driver 595.71.05, two independent problems stop the CUDA/GPU-attach path from working:

1. **PTX ISA version floor.** `rewrite_ptx_target()` (`attach/nv_attach_impl/nv_attach_utils.cpp`) stamps PTX `.version 8.5` for every `sm_num >= 100`. But `sm_103` was introduced in CUDA 12.9 = **PTX ISA 8.8**, so `ptxas` rejects every injected kernel with a version-too-low error.

2. **CUDA 13 `cuCtxCreate` signature.** CUDA 13 remaps `cuCtxCreate` to the 4-argument `cuCtxCreate_v4` (`CUcontext*, CUctxCreateParams*, unsigned int, CUdevice`). Three call sites (`nv_attach_impl.cpp` ×1, `runtime/src/handler/map_handler.cpp` ×2) use the old 3-argument form and fail to compile on CUDA 13.

Together these mean device-side eBPF can neither be built nor have its PTX compiled on a GB300. A PR fixing both follows (extends #541, which introduced the `rewrite_ptx_target` version ternary).

Environment: GB300 (`sm_103`), 2× Grace Neoverse-V2 aarch64, CUDA 13.2, driver 595.71.05, kernel 6.14.0-1013-nvidia-64k, LLVM 18, bpftime `master` @ `f311de2`.
