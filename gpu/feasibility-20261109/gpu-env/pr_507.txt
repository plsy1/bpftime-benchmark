**Related issue: #506** 

## Thoughts of modification:

Originally, when bpftime hooked CUDA kernels, it directly called cudaHostRegister on the CommSharedMem/IPC buffer within the nv_attach_impl code, and called cudaHostUnregister after the attach was completed.

Current implementation: When the agent constructs bpftime_shm with SHM_OPEN_ONLY, it calls register_cuda_host_memory(). It first executes cudaSetDeviceFlags(cudaDeviceMapHost), then performs a single cudaHostRegister on the entire Boost managed_shared_memory segment (segment.get_address()/segment.get_size()), and calls cudaHostUnregister upon destruction, keeping the CommSharedMem/IPC buffer entirely pinned.

Next, CUDAContext::CUDAContext() uses cudaHostGetDevicePointer to map the pinned segment into a GPU-visible address and writes it into cuda_shared_mem_device_pointer; finally, in bpf_attach_ctx::instantiate_bpf_link_handler_at(), nv_attach_impl::create_attach_with_ebpf_callback(), and fatbin_record::try_loading_ptxs(), this device pointer is passed to the NV attach in one go and written into the PTX's constData/map_info. 

This way, the entire bpftime path reuses that single cudaHostRegister from the shared segment initialization, eliminating the extra overhead of individual registration/unregistration for each probe.
